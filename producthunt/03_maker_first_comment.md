# Maker first comment (post right after launch)

Hi Product Hunt! I'm Shahariar, the maker of Notely AI. 👋

I built Notely because I kept coming out of lectures and meetings with hours of audio and almost
no usable notes. I wanted one place where I could drop a recording and get back a transcript, a
summary, action items, and, for students, flashcards and practice questions.

What's in it today:
- Transcript with speaker labels, summary and action items
- Study Mode (notes, flashcards, MCQs) and Business Mode (minutes, decision log, follow-ups)
- Translation, natural-language search across past meetings, and "Ask Notely" chat
- TXT / DOCX / PDF export, including Bengali in PDFs
- A free Windows recorder that captures mic + system audio for Zoom, Meet and online classes

What it doesn't do yet: calendar integration, live captions, an auto-join bot and mobile apps are
on the roadmap, not shipped. It's free with daily limits while I watch costs.

I'd love your honest feedback, especially:
1. Which output do you use most: summary, action items, or study pack?
2. What breaks on your audio (accents, noisy rooms, long files)?
3. Which language(s) should I test more?

Thanks for trying it. I'll be here all day answering questions.

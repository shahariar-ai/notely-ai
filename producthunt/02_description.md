# Full description

**Notely AI turns recordings of meetings and lectures into something you can use.**

Upload an audio file (mp3, wav, m4a) or record straight from your browser, and Notely AI:

- Transcribes it with speaker labels (and uses real names when they are spoken)
- Summarizes it: executive summary, key highlights, action items
- **Study Mode:** lecture notes, key concepts, flashcards and MCQs
- **Business Mode:** meeting minutes, executive report, decision log, follow-ups
- Translates summaries (Bengali, English, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Hindi, Chinese, Japanese)
- Lets you search your past meetings in plain language, and chat with them ("What did we decide about the budget?")
- Lets you share a meeting with a teammate by email
- Exports to TXT, DOCX and PDF (the PDF handles Bengali text)

**Recording Zoom, Meet or an online class?** The free Notely Recorder for Windows captures your
microphone and the system audio together, saves an MP3, and you upload it to Notely AI.

Free to use, with daily limits. Your data stays yours: delete it any time from the Privacy page.
Audio is processed by Google Gemini and stored in Supabase.
